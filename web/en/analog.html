—

The dial has ten major marks, but the day still lasts 100 new hours. The four digit hands move to the next mark only when their digit changes. At 57 hours and 24 minutes, they therefore point exactly to 5, 7, 2 and 4. The second hand sweeps: one full revolution takes 8.64 conventional seconds. In the paused example it shows 12 new seconds, just after mark 1.
